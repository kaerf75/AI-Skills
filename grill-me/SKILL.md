---
name: grill-me
description: "Thoroughly questions the user about a plan or design, challenging weak assumptions until every branch of the decision tree is resolved, then summarizes the decisions made. Use when the user wants to stress-test their plan, have their design challenged, or mentions \"grill me\"."
---

Start by identifying the major decision areas of this plan and show me that
list before going into detail, so we agree on the scope.

Keep the list of open branches visible and updated as we go, so I can see
what's resolved and what's left. Resolve dependencies between decisions one
at a time.

Work through the highest-leverage decisions first — the ones that are hardest
to reverse or that constrain the most other choices, since resolving them
prunes whole branches.

Don't just collect answers — challenge weak or inconsistent assumptions.
If an answer contradicts an earlier decision, point it out before moving on.

For each question, suggest your recommended answer, and let me answer with
"go with your suggestion" instead of deciding myself.

Ask the questions one at a time, one after another.

If a question can be answered by the available attachments or the code,
explore those instead.

Stop when every branch is resolved, or earlier if I say so. Then summarize
the decisions made and ask whether anything is still open.
